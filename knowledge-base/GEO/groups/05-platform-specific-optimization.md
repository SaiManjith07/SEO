# Platform-Specific Optimization

## Overview

The functional group covering a fact most GEO advice ignores: engines are not interchangeable. Each has a distinct source bias, and a single one-size-fits-all strategy will underperform on at least two of the major platforms.

## How it works

| Platform | Source preference | What actually wins here |
|---|---|---|
| **Google AI Overviews** | Authoritative, established sites — ~76% of citations come from pages already in Google's top 10 | Classic SEO. No shortcut — you must win `../../SEO/README.md` first |
| **ChatGPT** | Publishers, media, Reddit — leans on high-authority media (median DR ~90) and licensing-deal partners | Earning third-party media coverage and Reddit presence — your own site is not the main lever |
| **Perplexity** | Traditional search results — most Google-aligned; ~28.6% of citations rank in Google's top 10 | Same fundamentals as classic SEO, slightly less strict |
| **Google AI Mode** | YouTube and social — cites YouTube by a wide margin; cites Quora ~3.5x more than AI Overviews | Video and community presence — a text-only strategy is structurally capped here |

## Standards it touches

Cross-cutting — this group is a lens on how to weight effort across STD-01–03 (access), STD-26–29 (off-site), not a standard of its own.

## Tools & what to check

The AI visibility panel (`04-ai-visibility-panel.md`) segmented by platform — sample the same query set separately per engine, don't average across platforms into one number.

## Key metrics / thresholds

Reassess platform mix quarterly — this landscape moves fast (Gemini +231%, Claude +320% YoY). A strategy tuned to 2025's platform shares is already stale.
