# Video / YouTube Presence

## Overview

The functional group covering GEO's most underused high-ROI channel. YouTube is the **most cited domain in Google's AI Overviews**, and the correlation between YouTube mentions and ChatGPT visibility is **0.737** — higher than general brand mentions (0.664).

## How it works

```
Models process the AUDIO TRANSCRIPT, not just on-screen text or the title
        │
        ▼
Say the target phrase OUT LOUD in the video — spoken, not just shown
        │
        ▼
Upload a CORRECTED transcript rather than relying on auto-captions
        │
        ▼
Detailed, timestamped descriptions and accurate chapters
        │
        ▼
Structure the video answer-first — same BLUFF pattern as written
content (see ../../AEO/groups/01-answer-structuring.md)
        │
        ▼
Repurpose existing top content into video — low incremental cost,
high channel diversification
```

STD-29 (YouTube / video presence) — `../../standards/07-off-site-authority.md`

## Tools & what to check

No dedicated tool measures this well beyond generic AI-visibility panels (`04-ai-visibility-panel.md`) — treat YouTube optimization as a production/editorial workflow, not a tool-checked standard. Verify transcript accuracy manually against the auto-generated version; correct it rather than accepting the default.

## Key metrics / thresholds

- **0.737 correlation** between YouTube mentions and ChatGPT visibility — the single highest correlation figure in this knowledge base
- For most B2B and technical brands: a modest YouTube presence is described as **the single highest-ROI unexplored GEO channel** (`../../../research/03-GEO-generative-engine-optimization.md` §4)
