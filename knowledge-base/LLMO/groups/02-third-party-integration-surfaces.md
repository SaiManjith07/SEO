# Third-Party Integration Surfaces

## Overview

The functional group covering the part of LLMO that is genuinely distinct from SEO/AEO/GEO content work: relationships and business development, not page properties. This is a watch list, not a dashboard — there's no page-level check to run here.

## How it works

```
Track and pursue, on an ongoing basis:
  (a) inclusion in vendor "GPT Store" / custom-GPT ecosystems for
      your category
  (b) enterprise copilot visibility — if B2B, whether Microsoft 365
      Copilot or similar surfaces your product docs inside a
      customer's internal Copilot
  (c) publisher licensing deals — model vendors periodically license
      content directly from select publishers (as OpenAI has done);
      these control training-data terms directly rather than leaving
      it to scraping
```

These directly control whether your content enters training data on **favorable terms**, rather than being scraped under the default terms covered in `01-training-corpus-access.md`.

## Standards it touches

STD-32 (Third-party LLM integration surfaces) — `../../standards/08-llmo.md`. Explicitly not a build-time or page-level check.

## Tools & what to check

No tool tracks this — it's manual monitoring of vendor announcements, category-relevant GPT/plugin directories, and direct outreach where relevant (analyst-relations-style tracking, not SEO tooling).

## Key metrics / thresholds

None numeric. Track as a strategic initiative: presence/absence in relevant ecosystems, reviewed quarterly alongside the broader content strategy review.
